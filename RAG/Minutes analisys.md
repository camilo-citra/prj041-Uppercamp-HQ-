## Raw minutes
Each "minutes" file in RAW folder is a summary of a meeting.
RAW folder contains all the meetings.
The meetings order should follow the date recorded on the meeting

Meeting file contains the following important information:
	1. Meeting metadata
		1. date of the meeting
		2. link to transcript from gemini
		3. Team
	2. Executive summary
		1. the first meeting summarized the project brief
	3. Agenda Items discussed
		1. the agenda items may vary from meeting to the next meeting
		2. the project briefing happens in the first meeting 
		3. the agenda items are a record of the discussions and should be grouped by subject for future reference
		4. if similarities are recorded on between subjects, the discussions should be linked
	4. Decisions Taken
		1. this is record of the decisions that update the direction of the projects
		2. the decisions can affect Brief,  budget, process, specifications, responsibilities, task allocation for example
	5. Action Items
		1. immediate action task assigned to a team member, with a due date
	6. Risk Raised
		1. risk may contain contingency measures
		2. classification impact of low risk, medium risk high risk
		3. likely to happen clarification is low, medium or high
		4. risk analysis should exclude action items (action items are tracked separately under Action Items)
	7. Assumptions register
		1. this section records the assumptions taken at the time of the meeting based on the information at the time
		2. assumptions can be adjusted in new meeting records
		3. assumptions should be grouped on subject, trade, brief section

## Database and RAG management:

The meeting records feed the databases created for each tracking element.
on each meeting the database should be adjusted 

The base databases are:
	1. Brief
	2. Meeting metadata
	3. Decisions taken
	4. Actions items
	5. Risk Raised
	6. Assumptions
