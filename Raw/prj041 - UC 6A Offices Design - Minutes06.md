### **1\. Meeting Objective / Purpose**

The objective of the meeting was to brief the consultant on the electrical scope for two separate project sites (an office building and a factory) and to align on the project governance, required designs, and compliance sign-offs.

### **2\. Executive Summary**

The meeting established that the office and factory projects will be managed and invoiced separately as they operate on different electrical connections and timelines. The office building requires an occupancy reclassification and formal HVAC/fire designs for council approval. The factory site needs specialized electrical upgrades due to a dusty environment, including emergency lighting and smoke detectors. It was clarified that an electrical engineer must provide designs and sign-offs for council, while the electrical contractor will execute the work and issue the Certificate of Compliance (COC).

### **3\. Agenda Items & Discussion Notes**

* **Office Building Scope**:  
  * The project is moving from concept to design development. Phase 1 focuses on the ground and first floors; Phase 2 includes the third floor, roof, and a future fire escape.  
  * Required upgrades include electrical points, lighting, smoke detection, and HVAC systems.  
  * The building's occupancy designation must change from F1 to G1 (office). Formal HVAC and fire designs are mandatory for council compliance.  
  * A demolition process for the roof will begin in two weeks to prepare for a concrete slab and E-port installation. Load coordination with the HVAC engineer (Peter) is needed.  
* **Factory Site Scope**:  
  * The factory site layout is not yet fixed.  
  * The environment is dusty (cutting EPS), requiring three-phase power, isolators, emergency lighting, and smoke detectors.  
  * Extraction and ventilation must be addressed for HVAC functionality.  
* **Compliance and Sign-off**:  
  * An electrician will provide the COC for existing/new work.  
  * An electrical engineer is required to complete designs and documentation for council submissions.  
* **Governance and Invoicing**:  
  * Office and factory projects will be treated as separate scopes and invoiced independently via additional fees (not a new contract).  
  * The consultant is to provide an invoicing schedule aligned with the project timeline once the design development progresses.

## **Decisions Made**

* **Decision 1**: The new work scope regarding the office building and the factory will be managed as additional fees to the existing agreement, with invoices processed separately to maintain a clear distinction between the projects.  
* **Decision 2**: The electrical contractor is responsible for executing the installation and issuing the Certificate of Compliance (COC), while the engineer will provide the design and necessary documentation/sign-offs for council approval.
