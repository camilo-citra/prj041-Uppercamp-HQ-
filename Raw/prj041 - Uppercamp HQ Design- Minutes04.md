### **1\. Meeting Objective / Purpose**

Review the conceptual briefing and workspace strategy developed by Kim Williams Design for the new Citra Uppercamp head office (UC 6A), gain consensus on the behavioral/neighborhood framework (Connect, Build, Inspire, Community), and establish a phased implementation plan aligned with budget constraints. The meeting focused on the **architectural and interior design strategy** for the new Citra Head Office presenting the strategic behavioural framework and initial concept for discussion.

### **2\. Executive Summary**

The meeting focused on the architectural design strategy for the new campus headquarters, transitioning from traditional space allocation to an evidence-based behavioral framework composed of four core "neighborhoods": Connect (Ground Floor), Build (1st Floor), Inspire (2nd Floor), and Community (Rooftop). Due to budget considerations (target range R8M–R12M), execution will be executed in phases, prioritizing the Ground Floor and a partial section of the First Floor. Key discussions centered on layout optimizations (reception scale, boardroom placement, passage widths, hot-desking, desk size calibrations), structural/compliance constraints (fire escape staircases, elevator/disability requirements, sprinkler vs. secondary staircase costs), and building amenities (gym, showers, coffee stations, model workshop).

### **3\. Agenda Items & Discussion Notes**

* **Strategic Campus Design & Behavioral Framework**: Kim Williams presented seven strategic drivers (specialist collaboration, growth, culture, visible leadership, project life cycle, adaptive reuse, campus capability). Workspaces are categorized into modular neighborhoods (Connect, Build, Inspire, Community) rather than static offices.  
* **Implementation Phasing & Budget**: Construction will start on the Ground Floor and a partial section of the First Floor. Total current budget allocation discussed is between R8M and R12M.  
* **Ground Floor (Connect Neighborhood) Layout**:  
  * Functions as the central arrival point with a future-proofed reception for two receptionists, parcel storage, mirror-clad column, dual digital displays, and a living wall.  
  * Includes a 14-seater and 10-seater boardroom, acoustic booths, quiet/prayer room, hot-desking (12+ seats), and lunchroom/kitchen facilities.  
  * Cheryl Hillman proposed optimizing passage space around the 10-seater boardroom and replacing desk planter boxes with narrow screens to increase seating capacity.  
  * Accommodation schedule:  
    * 12 workstations (desking size 1m x 750\)  
    * 3 high desks  
    * 1 four-person office  
    * Collaboration lounge (8 people)  
    * Store/Delivery storge  
    * Two x four-seater meeting rooms  
    * Disabled cloakroom and shower  
    * Male and female ablutions (three cubicles each)  
    * Eight-seater collaboration lounge  
    * Coffee and recycling station  
  *   
* **First Floor (Build Neighborhood) Layout**:  
  * Houses technical execution, engineering, and construction teams with larger 1.4m desks for dual monitors and physical plans.  
  * Features an 8-person project room with a magnetic collaboration wall, 12-seater VC boardroom, A1/A3 printing & archive station, and acoustic focus booths.  
  * Ablution facilities are consolidated in one corner for simplified sewage routing.  
  * 24 workstations (1.4 m desks with dual monitors)  
  * Collaboration lounge  
  * Coffee and recycling station  
* **Second Floor (Inspire Neighborhood) & Rooftop (Community)**:  
  * 14-seater boardroom with floor-to-ceiling glass views for client/investor hosting, executive offices, model workshop/materials library, and EPOD strategy wall with mobile ottomans.  
  * 24 workstations (1.4 m × 750 mm desks)  
* **Rooftop** is planned as a community space with gardens, social areas, and potential EPOD placement.  
* **Structural, Compliance & Fire Safety Technicalities**:  
  * Debated a second fire escape staircase vs. a sprinkler system (costing \> R1M). Confirmation is required from fire consultant Peter regarding 1,200mm vs. 1,500mm passage widths and secondary staircase necessity.  
  * Clarified elevator requirements (accessibility / stretcher access) vs. retrofitting a home lift (R500k-R650k) or repurposing the shaft for a biophilic vertical wall or meeting booths.  
  * Third-party billboard advertising on the rooftop does not currently align with the Citra brand and will only be considered on this basis

## **Decisions Made**

* **Decision 1**: Project implementation scope is finalized to execute initially on the Ground Floor and a partial section of the First Floor based on budget allocation.  
* **Decision 2**: Replaced planter boxes between desks with narrow screens and overhead planting to maximize desk seating capacity.  
* **Decision 3**: Selected artificial foliage for overhead hanging plants to minimize ongoing maintenance and irrigation requirements.  
* **Decision 4**: Approved converting coffee area space to include dedicated library shelving and display cabinets for books and models.  
* **Decision 5**: Adopted glass walls for boardrooms to maximize natural light and floor-to-ceiling views.  
* **Decision 6**: Implemented magnetic write-on strategy/collaboration walls in project rooms and meeting areas.  
* **Decision 7**: Established policy prohibiting third-party advertising on the roof billboard space unless it generates significant high-value revenue. Advertising will only be considered where it aligns with the Citra brand. The roof windows are to be opened, and the parapet extended to accommodate any future advertising requirements.  
* **Decision 8**: Paused the design development phase for one week to collect detailed stakeholder feedback and consolidate briefing requirements. Additional feedback to be circulated by **Thursday next week**.

## **Issue Register**

* Issue: Existing staircase and passage dimensions do not clearly satisfy updated fire egress and disability access codes. **Root Cause**: Previous fire consultant retired and updated municipal regulations were not formally verified against current architectural drafts. **Proposed Solution**: Request urgent review by fire consultant Peter, and adjust passage widths to 1200mm/1500mm accordingly. **Note** that we are awaiting feedback from the fire consultant and, based on the regulations, anticipate that a second staircase will be required.  
* Issue: Unclear office allocation and physical location for Factory Manager and Commercial/Construction departments. **Root Cause**: Encroaching on current factory floor space before full factory layout design is completed risks disrupting operations. **Proposed Solution**: Postpone relocating Factory Manager office into the factory connection zone until comprehensive factory layout masterplan is finalized.  
* Issue: Lack of sufficient small meeting spaces and private call areas in current facility causing meeting room bottlenecks. **Root Cause**: Previous office design prioritized fixed desk count over collaborative and focus zones. **Proposed Solution**: Incorporate high-backed acoustic booths, 2-4 seater meeting pods, and focus phone booths across all floors.
