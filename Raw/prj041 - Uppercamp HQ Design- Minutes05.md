### **1\. Meeting Objective / Purpose**

To review the conclusions of the recent foundation studies, determine the structural support strategy for the roof and EPoD, and urgently coordinate the corrosion protection methodology for the lower levels.

### **2\. Executive Summary**

Foundation testing confirmed that internal column bases are insufficient to support the new roof structure. The team agreed on a strategy to bypass these internal columns entirely, instead utilizing steel girders to span across the existing, robust perimeter columns from the second floor upwards. This avoids new foundation work and keeps the lower floors clear for site activities. The immediate critical path involves finalizing a corrosion protection methodology (inhibitors vs. sacrificial anodes), with follow-ups required from Sovore and Zipex to prevent delays on the ground and first floors.

### **3\. Agenda Items & Discussion Notes**

* **Foundation Studies:** Test results showed positive bearing pressures for the perimeter, but internal column bases are too small and contain unknown (likely nominal) steel reinforcement.  
* **Structural Design Strategy:** Daniel proposed building off the existing "double columns" on the perimeter that terminate below the second floor. A steel structure and light slab will span from side to side, avoiding the need for any new columns, foundations, or slab perforations.  
* **Design Coordination:** Nonhlanhla and Camilo noted that structural work must align with EPoD spatial requirements. Daniel will provide preliminary sketches to clarify sizes and positioning so target ceiling heights and roof elevations can be verified.  
* **Lower Floor Impact:** Because all new structural work is contained from the 2nd floor up, ground floor site work can proceed safely without structural modifications.  
* **Corrosion Protection:** An urgent priority impacting the ground and 1st floors. Methodologies are being weighed (e.g., passing currents/sacrificial anodes vs. passive corrosion inhibitors).  
* **Concrete Reinstatement:** Confirmed as a minor 150mm slab replacement casting against the existing structure; no new reinforcement is required.

## **Decisions Made**

* **Decision 1**: The structural design strategy will bypass internal columns and utilize existing perimeter side columns to span the new structure.  
* **Decision 2**: The concrete repair methodology is confirmed as a 150mm slab replacement that will not require additional reinforcement.
