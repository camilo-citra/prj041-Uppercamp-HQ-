### **1\. Meeting Objective / Purpose**

To establish project governance, define team roles, agree on software collaboration protocols, and align on the strategic planning and design phases for the Upper Camp 6A construction project.

### **2\. Executive Summary**

The team officially kicked off the coordination for Upper Camp 6A, agreeing to a 16-week timeline targeting mid-January for completion. Project roles were clarified, with Camilo handling project management and budget approvals, and the design team leading architectural coordination. The team agreed to use Revit and the ACC platform for all documentation. A phased approach was established, emphasizing the need for formal stakeholder sign-offs at Stage 2 (planning principles) and Stage 3 (layout and cost estimates) to prevent design backtracking. Furthermore, the team aligned on the scope: the council submission will cover all three floors and the facade, but the internal fit-out will be limited to the first two floors, intentionally excluding major external construction like a lift upgrade.

### **3\. Agenda Items & Discussion Notes**

**Agenda Item 1: Project Governance, Roles, and Software Protocol**

* Meetings are set to occur bi-weekly on Thursdays.  
* Camilo Mogni will oversee project management and act as the primary channel for client-side decisions, specifically regarding budgets and allocations.  
* The design team will manage the architectural coordination and office space design.  
* The project will use Revit for modeling. Document sharing will take place on the Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (ACC) platform. Camilo will grant the engineering team access via the corporate group license to ensure seamless collaboration.

**Agenda Item 2: Scope Definition & Timelines**

* The timeline is estimated at 16 weeks, aiming for mid-January completion due to December holiday constraints.  
* The council submission will encompass all three floors, the building facade, the roof terrace, and the staircase.  
* The interior fit-out construction scope is strictly limited to the first two floors. External construction items, such as the lift, window replacement, and waterproofing, are excluded from the current budget.  
* The architectural team intends to design a high-quality facade for the council submission rather than settling for simple paint and plaster.

**Agenda Item 3: Design Workflow & Approval Process**

* Stage 2 will conclude with an approval on the planning direction (how space will be utilized and design limitations).  
* Stage 3 is a critical milestone where the layout and aesthetics must be formally signed off by Heads of Departments (HODs) to prevent costly backtracking during detailed design.  
* The team agreed to facilitate a presentation event with material samples at the end of Stage 3 to secure stakeholder alignment.

**Agenda Item 4: MEP, Fire Coordination, and Roof Layout**

* Fire, mechanical, and drainage systems will be planned holistically across all three linked buildings to ensure overall compliance.  
* HVAC ventilation options discussed: A fresh air system per floor vs. a centralized system on the roof. The centralized option requires structural consideration for core drilling through the slabs.  
* The placement of HVAC condensers and fans on the roof must be carefully mapped to minimize structural impact. The system currently requires approximately 12 mini systems.

### **4\. Decisions Made**

* **Decision 1 (Software):** Revit is officially selected as the primary modeling software, and the ACC platform will be used for document sharing.  
* **Decision 2 (Scope):** Council submission will include all three floors, facade, and roof, but interior fit-out construction is limited to the first two floors.  
* **Decision 3 (Budget Exclusions):** The existing lift will *not* be upgraded or replaced as part of the current budget/scope. Emergency exit procedures (e.g., chair escapes) will address safety compliance.  
* **Decision 4 (Sign-off Protocol):** Formal sign-off on the layout plan and cost estimates is mandatory at the end of Stage 3 to freeze the brief and prevent revisions.  
* **Decision 5 (Fire Strategy):** The fire safety strategy will be treated as a single, holistic submission across all three buildings.

### **5\. Risks, Issues, & Roadblocks**

* **Risk/Issue:** Financial surprises late in the design process due to a lack of early budget integration. (Status: Open)
  * **Mitigation/Next Step:** Cost estimations will be integrated by the end of Stage 3 so stakeholders understand expenditures before moving into the final concept phase.  
* **Risk/Issue:** Potential delays caused by a lack of stakeholder alignment or last-minute changes from HODs. (Status: Closed)
  * **Mitigation/Next Step:** Organize a dedicated presentation event at Stage 3 with physical material reviews to secure explicit buy-in before proceeding to detailed design.  
* **Risk/Issue:** Roof structural impact from heavy centralized HVAC equipment. (Status: Open)
  * **Mitigation/Next Step:** MEP to prepare dimensioned sketches of the 12 HVAC condensers and fans to ensure they integrate into the layout without interfering with structural columns.
