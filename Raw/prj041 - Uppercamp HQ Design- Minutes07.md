### **1\. Meeting Objective / Purpose**

To review infrastructure and architectural updates, finalize design capacity, establish the parallel submission strategy for building development approvals, and align on next steps for costing and council submission.

### **2\. Executive Summary**

The team reviewed updates on infrastructure (electrical and demolition) and safety requirements (fire safety and disabled access) following a meeting with the fire department. A key decision was made to reduce the building capacity to 90 people to ease compliance requirements. The team agreed on a parallel submission strategy, submitting land use applications (for departures) and BDM plans simultaneously. Preliminary technical drawings will be completed by early September to finalize costings before the demolition start date on September 28th. A pre-consultation meeting with BDM is planned to mitigate regulatory risks.

### **3\. Agenda Items & Discussion Notes**

* **Electrical and Infrastructure Update**: A large three-phase cable connected to the ground floor DB board must be integrated into the design as relocating it is too costly.  
* **Demolition Timeline**: Demolition starts September 28th and will take approximately three weeks to create a gray box.  
* **Fire Safety and Refuge Requirements**: The fire department mandates a lift and refuge areas on the second floor for the existing staircase. No waivers are allowed for this public building.  
* **Building Capacity**: The team agreed to reduce maximum occupancy from 130 to 90 people. This significantly reduces the requirements for fire escape passages, ablutions, and refuse areas.  
* **Ablution and Facility Planning**: The team plans to reduce ablution facilities to allow space for a gym on the second floor, utilizing the lowered capacity to motivate the reduction in toilets.  
* **Lift Strategy**: A lift is mandatory for disabled access to the roof. The team is investigating "pit-less" commercial lifts to avoid structural issues and will meet with contractors.  
* **Roof Terrace & Stairwells**: The fire department requires two stairwells to the roof for 90 people. The visual impact of the second stairwell on the "pod" design from the M5 highway is a concern; design options (e.g., curving the stairwell or lowering it) are being tested.  
* **Council Submission Strategy**: The team will pursue a concurrent submission process: a Land Use Management application for departures (e.g., encroachments on building lines) and a Building Development Management (BDM) application for the ground and first floors.  
* **Refuse Area**: The required refuse area for the site is currently inadequate. The team agreed to convert the planned outdoor kitchen space into a formal, enclosed refuse room to meet regulations.  
* **Costing & Timeline**: Preliminary technical drawings are needed to generate a Bill of Quantities (BoQ) for accurate costing. These will be ready by September 4th, with a final presentation scheduled for September 28th.

## **Decisions Made**

* **Decision 1**: Establish the building occupancy capacity limit at 90 people to reduce regulatory requirements for fire escapes, ablutions, and refuse.  
* **Decision 2**: Submit both the Land Use Management application and the Building Development Management (BDM) application simultaneously to run parallel processes.  
* **Decision 3**: Schedule the final presentation for project costing and design for September 28th.  
* **Decision 4**: Convert the area originally designated for an outdoor kitchen into a fully enclosed refuse storage room to satisfy council regulations.  
* **Decision 5**: Adopt a hot-desking model for HODs, reducing HOD office desks from six to four per space.
