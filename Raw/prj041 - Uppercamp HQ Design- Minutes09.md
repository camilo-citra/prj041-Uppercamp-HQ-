### **1\. Meeting Objective / Purpose**

The objective of this meeting was to coordinate municipal council submission sequencing, resolve administrative penalty regularizations, align sitewide utility infrastructure (sewer and stormwater), finalize refuse and ablution relocations across Buildings 6A and 6B, and coordinate structural and architectural designs for the rooftop EPOD/EOT installation and turnkey modular product rollout.

### **2\. Executive Summary**

The team established a multi-phased submission roadmap for the Uppercamp site. The foremost priority is submitting the sitewide administrative penalty application to regularize unauthorized works and process an occupancy conversion from industrial F1/D1 to temporary G1 occupancy, as municipal plan scrutiny cannot proceed without penalty clearance. Enrica Van Der Linden will lead the unified council submission for Building 6A (Middle Building) and Building 6B, incorporating relocated ablutions within Building 6B and a dedicated 10-bin enclosed refuse area behind the roller shutter of Building B. Nonhlanhla Mashego will manage council submissions and refuse compliance for Building 4 (the factory). Enrica will consult municipal health authorities regarding the existing sewer connection located on municipal land. Finally, Nicole Vivier and Kim Williams aligned on a parallel track to prepare Citra's turnkey modular EPOD product for a Q3 2027 market launch, with Kim providing interior finish and joinery specifications across three pricing and aesthetic tiers.

### **3\. Agenda Items & Discussion Notes**

* **Contractor Engagement & Construction Programming**: Kim Williams proposed introducing her building contractor to Camilo Mogni to quote and manage construction for the entire building structure rather than isolating the interior fit-out scope. The team agreed that combining the scopes offers commercial and project management efficiencies. Full-scale site construction is targeted for commencement in January 2027\.  
* **Council Submission Phasing & Administrative Penalty**: Nonhlanhla Mashego reviewed the site regularization drawings. An administrative penalty must be declared for the entire site to regularize prior unauthorized works and the shift to temporary G1 occupancy (supported by mechanical and fire compliance submissions). The team agreed on a three-phase submission pipeline: Phase 1 \= Sitewide Administrative Penalty; Phase 2 \= Building 4/Factory scrutiny submission (Nonhlanhla) and Ground/First Floors of Building 6A & 6B (Enrica); Phase 3 \= Top floor roof terrace and EPOD.  
* **Refuse and Ablution Relocation**: Per directives from leadership (Ral and Joel), the courtyard will be expanded, and the existing courtyard ablutions will be demolished and relocated inside Building 6B. The existing factory kitchen will remain and receive a sliding/stacking window to serve as an outdoor kitchen. The refuse area for Buildings B and C will be located behind the roller shutter of Building B (requiring an enclosed, rodent-proof room for 10 bins). Factory refuse will be retained within Building 4's scope.  
* **Site Infrastructure (Sewer & Stormwater)**: Review of the surveyor drawing revealed that the factory sewer manhole connects on municipal land rather than within the private property boundary. Enrica raised concerns regarding compliance risks for Building 6A plumbing. Enrica will engage City Health official Rory Cox with the surveyor PDF to resolve the connection status. Stormwater will continue discharging via existing rainwater downpipes into surface road channels leading to the Upper Camp Road curb inlet.  
* **EPOD Rooftop & Structural Coordination**: Nonhlanhla, Tymon, and Trevan coordinated the rooftop layout with structural transfer girders. The EPOD location was adjusted slightly to ensure compliant 1.2-meter egress clearances. The lift lobby exit will be lowered to align flush with the surrounding roof deck, and translucent materials will be investigated to reduce the visual bulk of the lift overrun. An in-person design workshop was set for Wednesday, September 16, 2026, at 1:30 PM.  
* **EOT / EPOD Product Development Roadmap**: Nicole Vivier introduced the commercial turnkey modular EPOD product strategy (targeted under 50 sqm, launching in Q3 2027 for vacation homes, retirees, and short-term rentals). Due to the delivery timeline precluding the proprietary Citra foundation, the entry-level price has been adjusted from under R1.0M to R1.5M. Kim Williams will review the product brief, competitor marketing deck, and concept drawings to provide interior design, joinery, and material palette specifications across 3 tiers (low, medium, high) and 3 color/mood themes.

## **Decisions Made**

* **Decision 1**: The team agreed to evaluate Kim Williams's building contractor for the construction scope of the entire building rather than restricting work solely to interior fit-outs.  
* **Decision 2**: Approved the placement of the consolidated refuse area for Buildings B and C behind the roller shutter door of Building B, accommodating a 10-bin enclosed room.  
* **Decision 3**: Adopted a three-phase council submission sequence starting with the sitewide administrative penalty, followed by ground and first floors of Buildings 6A and 6B, and concluding with the top floor rooftop terrace and EPOD.  
* **Decision 4**: Assigned Enrica Van Der Linden to handle the consolidated architectural council submission for Buildings 6A and 6B, including internal ablutions, refuse integration, and courtyard adjustments.  
* **Decision 5**: Scheduled an in-person design coordination workshop for the building facade, roof terrace, and structural integration on Wednesday, September 16, 2026, at 1:30 PM.  
* **Decision 6**: Deferred the decision regarding lift mechanical specifications (pit lift requiring staircase modification vs. platform lift) pending Camilo Mogni's technical review of consultant documentation.

## **Issue Register**

* Issue: Unauthorized historical building additions and unpermitted occupancy conversion from industrial F1/D1 to temporary G1 across the site. **Root Cause**: Works and operational alterations completed prior to formalized municipal plan approval. **Proposed Solution**: File an all-inclusive sitewide administrative penalty and regularization application accompanied by mechanical and fire compliance layouts.  
* Issue: Factory sewer connection sits beyond property boundaries on municipal land without an on-site connection point. **Root Cause**: Historical utility infrastructure layout predating current municipal site boundaries. **Proposed Solution**: Enrica Van Der Linden to meet with municipal health authorities (Rory Cox) using detailed survey PDFs to confirm connection legitimacy.  
* Issue: Stairwell landing and waterproofing compliance clash at the open third-floor roof terrace exit. **Root Cause**: An open-to-air stair design requires larger door landing clearances and water protection around the lift shaft. **Proposed Solution**: Lower the lift landing deck flush with the roof terrace and refine landing dimensions during the upcoming joint Revit design workshop.
